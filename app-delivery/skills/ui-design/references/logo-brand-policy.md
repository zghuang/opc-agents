# Logo And Brand Policy

When a selected template or user input includes an approved logo or brand asset:

- preserve it in `docs/ui/design-system.md`
- specify placement, scaled height, clear space, and aspect-ratio rules
- default placement is the header brand slot or sidebar brand rail
- do not infer broad brand rules from one screenshot when manifest/notes say otherwise

Use one dominant shell color for navigation chrome. Avoid competing saturated sidebar/header/primary navigation colors unless the requirements explicitly demand that style.
